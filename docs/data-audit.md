# Data audit — `Data Trip Angber (updated).xlsx`

Dataset version `DSV-20260916-b2354510` (workbook of 2026-09-16, 5.2 MB, 20 sheets). What it
contains, what the pipeline makes of it, and the decisions that were taken. Numbers are from
`data/tidy/DSV-20260916-b2354510/report.md`, which is regenerated by `fpt tidy`.

## What became training data

**400 operations** over six units, February–August 2026:

| Unit | Type / group | Rows | Modes | L/km (median) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Truck Crane 01 | Crane | 89 | all transport + lifting | 1.6 |
| Oil Field Truck (sheet `TrontonWinch`, `Angber` = `OFT Tronton`) | Truck | 87 | transport | 0.67 |
| Truck Crane 02 | Crane | 82 | all transport + lifting | 1.2 |
| Wheel Crane (sheet `Whellcrane`) | Crane | 61 | transport + lifting | 3.0 |
| Prime Mover | Truck | 61 | transport | 1.1 |
| Winch Truck (`Angber` = `OFT Winch Truck`) | Truck | 20 | transport | 1.1 |

Litres issued track distance well for the trucks (correlation 0.70–0.96 per unit) and not at all
for the truck cranes (≈0), whose day is dominated by lifting hours. That is the shape a model has to
capture: per-group slopes on distance *and* hours, which is what `baseline-v3` is for. A single
shared slope (`baseline-v2`) will fit the trucks and average the cranes.

**368 rows quarantined**, each with its reason in `quarantine.csv`:

| Reason | Rows | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `standby` | 157 | `Kegiatan` = STANDBY. Not an operation. Some carry litres — worth knowing, not learning. |
| `fuel_without_work` | 107 | Litres issued, no distance, no hours. **Almost all of March**: that month the sheets recorded litres and activity text only. 19 of these rows have a fuel-stick mileage instead; not enough to justify mixing a second distance source into the label's rows. |
| `no_fuel_issued` | 96 | Blank or zero litres with some work recorded. Nothing to predict. |
| `implausible_value` | 7 | A year typed into a numeric cell (`2026` km, `2026` h) and one 130-hour day. Limits: 1,000 km, 24 h. |
| `vehicle_unknown` | 1 | `TrontonWinch` row with a blank `Angber`. |

## Decisions

1. **The label is litres issued** (`Liter`), per ADR 0002. Fuel-stick consumption is joined for the
   report only. Where both exist, measured consumption is roughly half of what is issued (e.g.
   Truck Crane 01: 68 L issued vs 33 L measured on the same days; Wheel Crane 114 vs 81). That gap
   is the calibration question, deferred by design; it is not fixed by swapping the label.
2. **Activity mode is derived from the numbers**, not the free text: hours > 0 and km > 0 →
   `transport_and_lifting`; hours only → `lifting`; km only → `transport`. `Kegiatan` is kept as
   `activity_text` for a human to check.
3. **Distance is `D (Km)` only.** Fuel-stick mileage rides along as `fuel_stick_km`.
4. **Names resolve through production's catalog** (the same resolver the app uses at import and over
   MCP). `Whellcrane` → Wheel Crane, `OFT Tronton` → Oil Field Truck, `OFT Winch Truck` → Winch Truck
   are the only sheet-specific spellings, mapped once and asserted against the catalog on load.
5. **Stops are best-effort.** 397 rows have a trip text; 32 resolve completely. The rest name
   waypoints the catalog lacks — `tugu nanas`, `lingkar`, `pool cambai`, `simpang Pal 12`, `niru` —
   and a few genuinely ambiguous catalog entries (`SKG-001` vs `SKG-I`). Stops are not a model
   feature; they matter for the app's similar-history search, so this is a catalog task for the
   owner, listed in the report, not a blocker.
6. **Zero-padding was the app's bug.** `KRG-012` in the catalog never matched `KRG 12` in the sheets
   (or in a planner's message). Fixed in the app (`normalize_name`), which is why this repo depends
   on the app rather than copying its resolver.
7. **Time split, not random.** The last 20% of operations by date will be the test set (stage 3).

## What is not here, and why

- **The 13 vacuum trucks.** They have daily litres (`Fakta_BBM_Harian`) and fuel-stick readings but
  no trip sheets: no distance, no hours, no route. There is no *operation* to predict for them yet.
  Either the VT trip sheets exist somewhere and should be added to the workbook, or a coarser
  daily-litres model with route as the only feature is a separate decision. What `Data Ratio` does
  give is their **types** — Scania P410 6x6 (VT 01–03), UD Quester 6x4 (VT 04–08), Mitsubishi 5 t
  4x4 (VT 09), Scania P380 6x4 (VT 10–13) — which belong in production's catalog `tipe` column
  (ADR 0015) regardless of when a VT model exists.
- **2025.** `Fakta_BBM_Harian` covers 2025 at daily-litres level only; no operation rows exist for
  it.
- **Actuals as a label.** See decision 1.
